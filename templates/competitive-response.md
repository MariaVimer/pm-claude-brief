## Is this actually a threat?

Answer these before doing anything else.

**Which of your customers or prospects is this relevant to**: [specific segment — not "all customers"]
**How many of them**: [rough number or percentage of your base / pipeline]
**What problem does the competitor's move solve for them**: [in user language]
**Is it a problem you currently solve, partially solve, or do not address**: [FILL IN]

**The honest threat rating**:
- **High**: multiple customers have already asked about it, or it directly addresses a gap in your current offering that affects purchasing decisions
- **Medium**: relevant to a segment you care about, but you have time and a defensible position
- **Low**: relevant to customers you are not targeting, or a move in a space you have already decided not to compete in
- **Noise**: you are reacting to a press release, not customer behavior

---

## What you should not do

- React to a competitor announcement without talking to a customer first
- Build something to satisfy your sales team's anxiety rather than a real customer need
- Match a feature that customers do not actually use or value
- Treat "a competitor has it" as sufficient reason to build — that is the same as "because someone important asked for it"
