# CLAUDE.md — Go-to-Market

> Fill in every section before starting a session. Claude will challenge thin positioning, missing success criteria, and launches that cannot be defended on the merits.

---

## The hypothesis

> "We believe [audience] has [problem]. We believe [what we are launching] solves it better than the current alternatives because [specific differentiator]. We expect this to result in [outcome] within [timeframe]. We know this because [evidence]."

**Evidence behind this**: [name the source — user research, beta feedback, behavioral data, competitive analysis. "We think this will land well" is not evidence.]
**What would tell you the launch failed**: [name the threshold — not "low adoption" but specific numbers or behaviors]

---

## Market context

**How users solve this problem today**: [specific — workaround, competitor, or nothing, and what that costs them]
**What has changed in the market or in our product position that makes now the right time**: [FILL IN — if nothing has changed, ask whether this launch is reactive or strategic]
**Where competitors are**: [what they have shipped, what gaps remain, where we can win]
**Why a user would choose us over their current solution**: [be honest — if the answer requires a lot of conditions, your positioning will be weak]

---

## Audience

**Primary audience for this launch**: [specific person — role, company type, situation when they encounter this problem]
**How acute is their pain**: [daily / weekly / occasional — and the cost when it occurs]
**What they need to hear to change behavior**: [what makes them act, not just nod along]
**Secondary audience** (if any): [and how messaging differs for them]
**Who is NOT this launch's audience, even if they will see it**: [naming this protects against diluted positioning]

---

## The pain being solved

State the problem in user language, not product language.

**The specific situation where this pain occurs**: [FILL IN]
**What goes wrong, or what fails to happen**: [FILL IN]
**The consequence — for the user, their team, their business**: [FILL IN]
**What the user says about this problem** (if you have quotes): [real language from interviews or tickets]

---

## Positioning

**One-sentence positioning statement**: "For [audience] who [problem], [product] is the [category] that [benefit], unlike [alternative] which [limitation]."

**What we lead with** (the strongest true claim we can make): [FILL IN]
**What we do not lead with** (true but not differentiating, or a vulnerability): [FILL IN]
**The one thing we want someone to remember after seeing this launch**: [FILL IN]

---

## Why we are the right ones to solve this

**What we have that makes our solution better positioned than alternatives**: [distribution, data, integrations, trust, specific capability]
**What we are not**: [what this launch is not trying to be — important for scoping claims and avoiding overreach]

---

## Opportunity cost

**What we are not doing because we are doing this launch now**: [name it]
**Why this launch takes priority**: [FILL IN]

---

## Launch scope

**Channels**: [email / in-app / blog / PR / events / other — and why each one for this audience]
**What is NOT in scope for this launch**: [explicit — prevents scope creep in execution]
**Pricing and packaging** (if changed or new): [FILL IN or "no change"]
**Sales and CS enablement required**: [what they need to know to pitch and support this]

---

## How we will measure success

**Leading indicator** (what changes in user behavior within 30 days): [not "impressions" or "clicks" — what downstream behavior shows the problem is being solved]
**Lagging indicator** (what business outcome moves in 90 days if the launch worked): [FILL IN]
**Counter-metric** (what you are watching to ensure you are not breaking something else): [FILL IN]
**Learning threshold** (what result tells you the positioning or audience was wrong): [FILL IN]

---

## Constraints

**Launch date**: [hard / soft / target]
**What other teams own in this GTM**: [Marketing, Sales, CS — and their sign-off requirements]
**Decisions already made** (not up for debate): [FILL IN or "none"]

## Output spec

**What I need**: [positioning brief / launch checklist / customer email draft / sales one-pager / blog post / other]
**Format**: [FILL IN]
**Audience for the output**: [internal / external / specific role]
**Tone**: [FILL IN]

## Working style

**How I want Claude to respond**: [draft first / challenge positioning first / options then pick]
